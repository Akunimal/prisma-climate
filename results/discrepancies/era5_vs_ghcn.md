## Bias decomposition — t2m_max (ERA5 vs GHCN)

Sequential adjustments on the same overlapping days; each residual is the mean(ERA5 − station) after that step only:

1. **raw** — unadjusted difference
2. **elevation** — lapse-rate correction (6.5 °C/km) from the series' source elevation to station elevation
3. **sampling** — constant mean correction from a parabolic peak fit to recent hourly ERA5 (sub-hourly max missing from hourly-sampled daily max)
4. **day_convention** — best lag in {−1, 0, +1} days minimizing MAE

Limitation of step 3: the sampling correction is estimated once from a single recent 21-day window (per row: `sample_window`) and applied as a constant to the whole history — it is not re-estimated per season, so seasonal variation in sub-hourly peakiness is not captured.

Open-Meteo applies statistical downscaling to the 90 m DEM elevation of the requested point by default (docs: parameter `elevation`). Two tracks are therefore reported per location:

- **downscaled** — the stored connector series; source elevation = the DEM elevation the downscaling targeted (`dem_elevation_m`).
- **raw_grid** — refetched with `elevation=nan` (downscaling off); source elevation = average ERA5 grid-cell orography (`grid_elevation_m`).

## Honest reading

- An earlier version of this step used Open-Meteo's default `elevation` response field as "grid orography". That field is the 90 m DEM elevation used for statistical downscaling, not ERA5 model orography, so the previous elevation gap was not an ERA5 orographic signal. This report uses `elevation=nan` requests for real grid-cell heights.
- Data freshness (ok rows, as of 2026-11-13): ERA5 through 2026-11-05 (8 d old); GHCN through 2025-08-24 (446 d ≈ 15 months old) although both stores were refreshed 2026-11-11 — the gap is on the GHCN side, not a stale local cache. The overlap above ends at the GHCN end date, so recent-forecast verification in Phase 1 depends on the SMN Argentina connector until GHCN-Daily updates (audit: `results/qc/freshness.md`).
- All configured stations match the SMN list within 0.05° / 50 m, so the residual below is not attributable to a misplaced station coordinate in the region config.
- **downscaled** (n=19 stations): mean bias raw -2.91 °C → after elevation -2.93 °C → after sampling + day convention -2.88 °C.
- **raw_grid** (n=18 stations; 1 excluded): mean bias raw -3.74 °C → after elevation -2.97 °C → after sampling + day convention -2.93 °C.
- Track convergence: at all 18 location(s) with both tracks included, the final residual agrees within 0.014 °C (mean |Δ| 0.006 °C).
- Exception: raw_grid at Mar del Plata Aero is NOT part of that agreement (final -3.66 °C vs -1.97 °C downscaled, Δ -1.69 °C); its grid cell is water-masked (0 m) so the tracks read different cells. It is excluded from the raw_grid mean above, not averaged in.
- The residual cold bias persists on both series after all three adjustments. It is reported as an **observed ERA5 bias of these grid values at these stations**; no causal claim is made here.
- **Correction (2026-11-13):** the size of that residual is **leg-dependent**, not a single ERA5 constant. Recomputed with sign ERA5 − station over each leg's own paired days (`results/discrepancies/bias_reattribution.{csv,md}`): the GHCN leg (1996-01-01 → 2025-08-24) averages -2.91 °C on t2m_max with 9/19 stations at |bias| >= 3 °C, while the SMN leg (2025-11-11 → 2026-11-05) averages -0.97 °C with 0/19; the two windows share **0** station-days, so the contrast is period-confounded. Discriminating candidates — different periods (climatology/decade), different instruments and aggregation (GHCN record vs SMN capture served as-is), paired-day selection, and source QC/versioning — are bounded by the controls in `bias_per_year_ghcn.csv` (per-year) and `bias_matched_calendar.csv` (matched-calendar adjacent); none is asserted here as the cause.
- **Controls (2026-11-13):** the matched-calendar control (`bias_matched_calendar.csv`) still shows a mean t2m_max gap of -2.52 °C (-3.49 °C GHCN vs -0.97 °C SMN over adjacent same-span windows 2024-11-11..2025-08-24 vs 2025-11-11..2026-08-24, GHCN more negative at 19/19 stations, GHCN n_days 72–189 of 287 per station vs SMN 261–287), and the per-year control (`bias_per_year_ghcn.csv`) moves the station-mean ERA5−GHCN t2m_max bias from -2.21 °C (1996–2003) to -3.49 °C (2005–2025) while mean coverage drops from 271.8 days in 2003 to 169.3 days in 2005 — the 2004–2005 step coincides with that coverage drop — and mean coverage is 113.8 days in 2025 before the 2025-08-24 cutoff. Independently of those coverage figures, a pure daily-window shift cannot produce a ~2 °C mean bias. The model-splice candidate is ruled out by the two-request check (stored ERA5 matches a `models=era5` request exactly, while the same request without the parameter differs from the stored series).
